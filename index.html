FAQ

Common Questions

What does a car wash pay station do?

It is the point where customers pay for a wash, usually by card, cash or tokens, and it triggers the wash cycle. A good unit keeps transactions quick and secure.

Can you replace my existing unit?

Yes. We remove aging equipment, install a modern replacement and make sure it works with your current wash setup.

Do you offer repairs and maintenance?

Yes. We provide emergency repairs as well as preventative maintenance plans to reduce unexpected downtime.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Fort Myers, North Fort Myers and communities throughout Southwest Florida.
